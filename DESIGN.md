1. Color Palette (Sistem Warna)
Gunakan Slate sebagai warna dasar netral (agar tidak terlalu kontras seperti pure black/white) dan Indigo sebagai warna aksi (Primary Accent).

🎨 Base Neutral (Slate)
Background Utama (Page): bg-slate-50 (#f8fafc)

Surface/Card: bg-white (#ffffff)

Border Standard: border-slate-200 (#e2e8f0)

Border Soft: border-slate-100 (#f1f5f9)

Muted / Placeholder / Skeleton: bg-slate-200 (#cbd5e1)

🎨 Typography Neutral
Text Primary (Headings, Titles): text-slate-900 (#0f172a)

Text Secondary (Subtitles, Labels): text-slate-600 (#475569)

Text Muted (Placeholders, Captions): text-slate-400 (#94a3b8)

🚀 Brand & Primary Accent (Indigo & Electric Violet)
Primary Main (Buttons, Active States): bg-indigo-600 (#4f46e5)

Primary Hover: hover:bg-indigo-700 (#4338ca)

Primary Soft / Badge Background: bg-indigo-50 (#e0e7ff)

Primary Soft Text: text-indigo-600 (#4f46e5)

🏷️ Product Category Badges (Aset Digital)
Canva Templates: bg-pink-50 text-pink-600 border-pink-200

Excel / Spreadsheet: bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-600 border-emerald-200

UI & Design Assets: bg-violet-50 text-violet-600 border-violet-200

2. Shadows & Elevation
Untuk memberikan efek kartu melayang (floating card) yang halus:

Card Shadow: shadow-sm shadow-slate-200/50

Card Hover Shadow: hover:shadow-md hover:shadow-indigo-500/10

Sticky/Navbar Shadow: shadow-sm backdrop-blur-md bg-white/80

3. Typography & Micro-Interactions
Primary Font: Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans (font-sans)

Rounded Radius:

Component/Button: rounded-xl (12px)

Container/Card: rounded-2xl (16px)

Badges: rounded-full atau rounded-lg (8px)

Transitions: transition-all duration-200 ease-in-out
